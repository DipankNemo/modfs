## 1. The three figures, side by side

| | whole-catalogue ratio | small | large | document | line | catalogue | measured | calibration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---|---|
| A | **2.51×** | 5.59× | 1.32× | `docs/STATE_OF_PLAY_2026-09-18.md` | 162 | 38 modules (31 small + 7 large) | 2026-09-16 | none |
| A′ | **2.51×** | 5.59× | 1.32× | `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 | 672–674 | same as A | same as A | none |
| B | **1.84×** | 5.59× | 1.20× | `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 re-measure block | 684–686 | 40 modules (31 small + 9 large) | 2026-09-19 | none |
| B′ | **1.84×** | 5.59× | 1.20× | `thesis/PLAN.md` | 24 | same as B | same as B | none |
| C | **2.47×** | — | — | `docs/REASSESSMENT_2026-09-10.md` §8.2 | 522 | 37 modules | 2026-09-10 | ×0.993 applied |

All five rows use the **same formula and the same method**:

    stored     = B + Σd                    one base, N sibling deltas
    monolithic = N·B + Σd                  one whole image per use case
    ratio      = monolithic / stored

in **decimal MB (10⁶)**, with `B` = 41 717 760 B = 41.7 MB. There is no
methodological disagreement between them at all.

## 2. Which is canonical

**B / B′ — 5.59× small, 1.20× large, 1.84× whole catalogue, 40 modules — is
canonical as of today (2026-09-21).**

It is canonical because it is the only one whose catalogue is the catalogue that
exists. `scripts/16_build_evidence.sh` recomputes it from the `.sqsh` files on
disk on every run, and table **S5** of `storage.md` shows it reproducing to the
published decimal. It is the figure that will move when the GPU modules are
rebuilt or re-swept, because nothing about it is transcribed.

A and A′ are **not wrong**; they are the same measurement of a catalogue that
has since grown. S5 reconstructs their exact catalogue — all 40 modules minus
`nvidia-driver-535` and `cuda-runtime` — from today's artefacts and recovers
`5.59× / 1.32× / 2.51×` to the decimal. That reproduction is what licenses
calling them *superseded* rather than *unverified*.

C is **not reconstructible**. Its 37-module generation was overwritten by the
full rebuild of 2026-09-16, so no combination of artefacts now on disk yields
its Σd of 966 459 392 B. It survives only as the byte counts written down inside
REASSESSMENT itself. S5 records it as `NOT REPRODUCIBLE` rather than silently
approximating it.

## 3. Why each pair differs

### 3.1 A → B (2.51× → 1.84×) is catalogue composition, not method

This is a **result, not an error**. Two real modules were added on 2026-09-19:

| module | stored | effect on Σd | effect on N·B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `nvidia-driver-535` | 231.6 MB | +231.6 MB | +41.7 MB |
| `cuda-runtime` | 680.2 MB | +680.2 MB | +41.7 MB |
| | | **+911.8 MB** | **+83.4 MB** |

The benefit of the delta model is `B·(N−1)/(B+Σd)`. A module contributes `B` to
the numerator and its own `d` to the denominator, so a module larger than the
base **lowers** the aggregate ratio by construction. `cuda-runtime` is 16.3× the
size of the base and shares nothing with its siblings beyond base itself; per
module it returns 5.8 %, against over 99 % for `nc-traditional`.

The small cohort is **identical in membership and value** (31 modules, 5.59×)
across A and B. That makes it a control on the re-measurement: the machinery
gave the same answer for an unchanged input, so the change in the whole-catalogue
figure is attributable to the two new modules and not to a change in how the
measurement was taken.

The sensitivity is worth reporting in its own right, and `storage.md` **S4**
tabulates it. Removing `cuda-runtime` alone takes the headline from 1.84× to
2.26×; the nine large modules are **87.8 % of all delta bytes** while being
9 of 40 modules. **The headline ratio is a property of the catalogue at least as
much as of the method**, and any single number quoted without its catalogue is
uninterpretable.

### 3.2 C → A (2.47× → 2.51×) is two changes, neither of them method

Decomposing it exactly:

| step | ratio | what changed |
|---|---:|---|
| C as published | 2.47× | 37 modules, calibrated ×0.993 |
| remove the calibration | 2.49× | 37 modules, uncalibrated — the figure `JOURNAL.md` records as "was 2.49×, N=37" |
| add the 38th module | 2.51× | 38 modules, uncalibrated = A |

So **0.02 of the gap is the calibration and 0.02 is the extra module.** Neither
is a disagreement about how to compute the ratio.

C is the **only** one of the three that applies the ×0.993 monolithic
calibration. It is therefore not comparable with A or B as printed: comparing
them requires either adding the calibration to A and B or removing it from C.
Today's 38-module figure calibrated is 2.49×, uncalibrated 2.51× — the two are
reproduced side by side so the choice is visible rather than buried.

## 4. Was the monolithic baseline rebuilt like-for-like, or extrapolated?

**Extrapolated, in all three — with a six-point calibration.** This is the part
most at risk of being overstated in the thesis, so it is worth stating flatly.

- The monolithic column is **modelled** as `B + d` for **every** module, in A, B
  and C alike. `scripts/13_storage_ratios.sh` and `scripts/16_build_evidence.sh`
  both compute it that way and both say so.
- **Six** real monolithic images exist and were rebuilt on 2026-09-16 alongside
  the deltas: `curl`, `emacs`, `jq`, `nc-traditional`, `pytools`, `webserver`.
  Table **S2** compares each against its model. The model lands **0.25–0.95 %
  high** on all six — squashfs compresses one whole tree slightly better than a
  base and a delta compressed separately — so the model **mildly overstates the
  saving**, by under 1 %, in a known direction.
- For the whole-catalogue column that means **6 measured figures and 34 modelled
  ones**, not 40 rebuilt baselines.

Two consequences follow, and both belong in the thesis:

1. **`thesis/PLAN.md` line 24–25 overstates this.** It says the storage figure
   comes "with the monolithic baseline rebuilt like-for-like". Six of forty were
   rebuilt like-for-like. The sentence is true of the calibration and false of
   the baseline. Listed in `inconsistencies.md` as I-14.
2. **The calibration is unvalidated over the range that now dominates.** The six
   real monoliths span deltas of 0.3 MB to 37.0 MB. The nine large modules span
   56.6 MB to 680.2 MB and carry 87.8 % of all delta bytes; `cuda-runtime` alone
   is **18.4× the largest delta the calibration was measured on**. Nothing here
   shows the 0.7 % bias holds at that size, and nothing shows it does not. The
   honest statement is that the whole-catalogue monolithic column is an
   extrapolation calibrated on the small end of the distribution and applied
   across the whole of it. This is the concrete form of **ARCHITECTURE §10 H9**,
   "the monolithic comparison path is not a controlled equivalent baseline",
   which is still open.

Closing it needs one real monolithic build of a large module. The cheapest
decisive one is `gcc` (84.0 MB delta, 125.7 MB modelled); the most informative
is `cuda-runtime` (680.2 MB delta, 721.9 MB modelled), which is the module
actually driving the headline. The commands are in §7 of the covering report.

## 5. What may and may not be claimed

**May be claimed**, from the artefacts on disk today:

- 5.59× / 1.20× / 1.84× for the 31-module small cohort, the 9-module large
  cohort and the 40-module catalogue, on a base of 41.7 MB, decimal MB, with the
  monolithic side modelled as `B + d`.
- That the model overstates the saving by 0.25–0.95 % across six real monolithic
  builds spanning 0.3–37 MB of delta.
- That the ratio fell from 2.51× to 1.84× **because two large modules were
  added**, and that this is the formula behaving as stated.
- That the saving is **server-side storage and assembly**: `11_boot_test.sh`
  flattens the composed overlay into a conventional ext4 root, so the node
  receives an ordinary image.

**May not be claimed:**

- That the whole-catalogue monolithic baseline was rebuilt like-for-like.
- That the 0.7 % calibration holds for modules an order of magnitude larger than
  any on which it was measured.
- That 2.47×, 2.51× and 1.84× are competing estimates of one quantity. They are
  one estimator applied to three different catalogues, two of which no longer
  exist.
- Any single ratio without naming its catalogue size and date.
